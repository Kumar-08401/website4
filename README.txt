GANESH PLACEMENT AGENCY WEBSITE FILES

Files:
- index.html: website markup
- styles.css: website styling
- script.js: navigation, hero slider, KPI counters, and About slider

Setup:
1. Keep index.html, styles.css, and script.js in the same folder.
2. Keep your existing images folder beside these files.
3. Open index.html in your browser (or use VS Code Live Server).

Important:
- The HTML expects the image files under images/slides/slide-2.jpg and images/slides/slide-3.jpg, plus your existing images/icons and images/collages files.
- The navigation links are connected to the sections currently present in your HTML: Students -> Services, Employers -> Work, Programs -> Skills, Colleges -> Clients, About -> About slider, Contact -> Contact section. Rename or rewrite those sections as needed to match your final content.
- KPI numbers in the HTML are the values from your uploaded source. Verify them and replace them with accurate company figures before publishing.
- The source still contains some Prism-template marketing text and contact details in other sections; update those before publishing.
